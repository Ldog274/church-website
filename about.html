About Us

East Side Free Will Baptist Church has served Muldrow and the surrounding area for generations — a congregation with a long memory and an open door.

Visiting for the first time

The first visit is the hardest part, so here is what you need to know.

  • Where we are — 613 E Sequoyah St, Muldrow, OK 74948, a block east of the main road through town.
  • Sunday morning — Sunday school for every age at 9:30 AM, then morning worship at 10:30 AM.
  • Children — children’s church meets during the 10:30 AM worship hour, so parents can worship while the children are taught at their own level.
  • Wednesday — the church gathers at 7:30 PM for Bible study and prayer, and our youth meet at the same hour at the Activities Center, 1302 S. Main St..

If you have a question we have not answered here — about parking, about access, about anything at all — please call us at (918) 427-5116. We would far rather answer the phone than have you wonder.

Our pastors

Pastor Anthony Williams

Pastor Anthony Williams has served this church since 2001. In more than two decades here he has baptised, married, and buried much of this community, and he still preaches the Word plainly week by week.

Associate Pastor Logan Williams

Logan Williams has served as associate pastor since 2024. He is Pastor Williams’s son, and his work centres on the nursing home, hospital, and shut-in ministry — visiting the sick, carrying communion to those who cannot get out, and keeping the church connected to members who are no longer able to be here.

A new worship and ministry center

In October 2025 the church broke ground on a 14,000 square foot worship and ministry center at 1302 South Main Street. It is expected to open in December 2026 and will give us room to gather, teach, and host the community in a way the current building cannot.

What we are about

We are ordinary people who believe the Bible is God’s Word, that salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, and that a church is at its best when it is caring for the people around it.

You will find us in the hospital room, at the nursing home, and at the kitchen table of somebody who is having a hard week — as much as you will find us on a Sunday morning.
