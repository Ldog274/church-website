Calendar

Services, youth, and upcoming events at East Side. This page is tied directly to the church’s Google Calendar, so anything added there appears here automatically — nothing to update by hand.

Every week

Sunday — Sunday School 9:30 AM · Morning Worship 10:30 AM (children’s church at the same hour)
Wednesday — Midweek service 7:30 PM · Youth worship 7:30 PM at the Activities Center, 1302 S. Main St.

Our church calendar is being connected to this page. In the meantime, the weekly pattern above is unchanged, and any special events are announced on our Facebook page.

What you will find on the calendar

  • Regular services and midweek gatherings
  • Youth events at the Activities Center
  • Fifth Sunday singings, fellowships, and special services
  • Christmas events, including the Tour of Christmas
  • Building progress milestones for the new worship and ministry center

Coming to something?

You do not need to tell us you are coming, and you will not be singled out. If you would like someone to look out for you, call the church at (918) 427-5116 and we will make sure you are expected.
