Announcements

Here is what we want you to know this week — services, classes, events, and life in the congregation.

Updated November 1, 2026

B.A.T.T.L. Ministry T-Shirt Sale

The B.A.T.T.L. Ministry has extended its T-shirt sale through Sunday, November 8. Shirts are $15 each. Please see Bro. Robert Pryor if you would like to purchase one. Thank you for supporting this important ministry!

Pure Vanilla Fundraiser

We have pure vanilla from Mexico available for purchase as a fundraiser for the Vision Fund. Each bottle is $10, just in time for holiday baking! Please stop by the church office if you would like to purchase some.

Church Workdays

Due to ongoing construction and our anticipated move into the new church in December, our annual TOC has been postponed until December 2027. However, we have scheduled two workdays to clean up the property surrounding our new church:

  • Saturday, November 7, at 8:00 AM
  • Sunday, November 15, at 1:30 PM

Final Construction Phase Push

We are holding a “Final Construction Phase Push” for the Vision Fund through December 2026. Our goal is to raise $200,000 by the end of the year. Please prayerfully consider contributing toward this goal as we prepare to move into our new church.

Mayor’s Prayer Breakfast

The Muldrow Ministerial Alliance’s “Mayor’s Prayer Breakfast” will be held on Thursday, November 12, at 9:00 AM at Muldrow Assembly of God Church. Please let Bro. Anthony know if you are interested in attending.

Thanksgiving Community Service

The Thanksgiving Community Service will be held at Trinity United Methodist Church on Sunday, November 22, at 6:00 PM.

Online Tithes and Offerings

If you would like to give your tithes and offerings online, please download the Church Center App or scan the QR code provided in the bulletin. Please see Sis. Myra if you have any questions or would like additional information. Visit our giving page for the current fund links.
